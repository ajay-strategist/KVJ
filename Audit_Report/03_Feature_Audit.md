# Feature Audit Matrix

This document provides a feature-by-feature evaluation of the KVJ Analytics application, grading implementations, risk levels, and identifying gaps between expected and actual system behaviors.

| Feature Area | Purpose | Implemented | Status | Priority | Risk Level | Dependencies | Expected Behavior | Actual Behavior / Audit Finding |
| :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- | :--- |
| **Auth & Identifier Resolver** | Allow users to log in with email, username, or phone number. | Yes | **Working** | Critical | High | Supabase Auth, `resolve_login_email` RPC, `flwdsk_employees` | Users enter any identifier; the system resolves the email and authenticates. | Working as expected, but includes hardcoded developer profiles and fallback passwords in `SupabaseAuthService.ts`. |
| **Attendance Logging** | Track employee clock-in, clock-out, break-in, and break-out states with geo coordinates. | Yes | **Partially Working** | Critical | Medium | Browser Geolocation, `flwdsk_attendance_records`, `flwdsk_work_sessions` | Clock status updates immediately; late arrivals are flagged; geo coordinates are saved. | Working on the frontend. However, the background cron to auto-close dangling sessions at 23:59 runs on the Express MongoDB backend, leaving dangling sessions orphaned in Supabase. |
| **Leave Approval Workflow** | Enforce Reporting Manager → CEO approval chain with role-based skip-step logic. | Yes | **Working** | High | Medium | `flwdsk_leave_records`, `flwdsk_employees` | Leave claims route to manager then CEO. If a manager files leave, it routes directly to the CEO. | Working as expected. The skip-step check and the manager-to-CEO chain function correctly. |
| **Student Roster Excel Upload** | Enable trainers/admins to upload xlsx student lists to populate batch rosters. | Yes | **Working** | High | Medium | `flwdsk_student_records`, `flwdsk_enrollments`, SheetJS | Uploading an Excel file parses and inserts student rows and enrollment linkages. | Working, but column formatting must be exact. Fails if the file structure diverges from expected keys. |
| **Student Eligibility Gate** | Enforce assessment-based final exam eligibility. | Yes | **Working** | High | Low | `flwdsk_assessments`, `flwdsk_student_records` | Students must score `>= 84%` on all selected assessments to become eligible. | Working. Trainers can select which assessments count as gates per batch. |
| **Exam Voucher Management** | Manually allocate exam vouchers from a bulk inventory pool. | Yes | **Working** | Medium | Low | `flwdsk_vouchers`, `flwdsk_enrollments` | Admins assign voucher codes to eligible students, marking voucher status. | Working as expected. |
| **Derived Travel Expenses** | Auto-calculate mileage cost based on distance and vehicle type. | Yes | **Working** | High | Low | `flwdsk_expense_claims`, `flwdsk_expense_types` | Self Travel claims compute cost = `km * ratePerKm[mode]` using CEO-managed rates. | Working. Formula computes values correctly, but defaults to rate seeds if the database is not seeded. |
| **3-Step Task Approval** | Manage project task progression through Supervisor → Manager → CEO approval. | Yes | **Working** | High | Medium | `flwdsk_tasks`, `flwdsk_task_work_sessions` | When a task is marked complete, it escalates through the hierarchy. | Working. Tasks show correct approval statuses and notes. |
| **Real-time Chat Channels** | Enable collaboration inside team, project, and training chat rooms. | Yes | **Partially Working** | Medium | Medium | Socket.io server, `flwdsk_chat_messages`, `flwdsk_chat_channels` | Users exchange messages in real-time, receiving instant notifications. | Messages are saved directly to Supabase. However, Socket.io is not connected on the frontend. The chat UI lacks live subscriptions, requiring page refreshes to pull messages. |
| **Announcements Board** | Publish company-wide announcements and holiday notices. | Yes | **Working** | Low | Low | `flwdsk_announcements`, `flwdsk_declared_holidays` | Admins publish notifications that appear on employee dashboard widgets. | Working as expected. |
| **Email Center & Queue** | Send notification emails via SMTP relay configurations. | Yes | **Partially Working** | Medium | Medium | `flwdsk_email_logs`, NodeMailer (Express) | Triggering notifications inserts logs; the backend dispatches SMTP relays. | Frontend successfully logs email records in `flwdsk_email_logs`. However, the NodeMailer daemon runs on the legacy Express/MongoDB server, leaving the Supabase email queue stagnant. |
| **Executive Analytics Console** | Provide managers and executives with aggregated KPI metrics and operational charts. | Yes | **Working** | Medium | Low | `flwdsk_kpi_definitions`, `flwdsk_saved_reports` | Visualizes aggregated summaries of attendance, tasks, and finance metrics. | Working, but largely relies on mock/stub calculations or basic SQL sums rather than dynamic OLAP queries. |
| **PowerBI Gateway** | Embed interactive PowerBI reporting frames. | Yes | **Missing / Flagged** | Low | Low | Feature Flag: `appConfig.integrations.powerBiEnabled` | Renders embedded frames when enabled. | Disabled via configuration. Displays a standard placeholder screen. |
